Your Step by Step Guide

Understanding your health shouldn't be complicated, and we believe regular bloodwork is one of the most important steps you can take toward better health.

Read more Read less

We encourage establishing a baseline before starting medication and checking in regularly, often every six months depending on your individual needs. It's not just about how you feel, but understanding what's happening inside your body and how your health or treatment may be affecting it.

Being able to see your progress, recognize meaningful changes, and make informed decisions gives you the confidence to take control of your health. We're here to make that process simple, approachable, and worth looking forward to.

Step 1Schedule your tests

Start with your baseline.

Choose the bloodwork panel that fits your needs. With options ranging from Basic to Comprehensive. Testing is available at Quest or at home, giving you the flexibility to select the setting that works best for you.

Basic Panel

Designed for routine health check-ins, with a focus on blood health, heart health, liver and kidney function, thyroid activity, blood sugar, and selected hormones. A starting point for establishing your baseline and tracking changes over time.

Advanced PanelRecommended

Includes everything in the Basic Panel, plus a deeper look at heart health, blood sugar regulation, hormone function, inflammation, and nutrient levels. Designed for those who want a more detailed understanding of how their body is functioning.

Comprehensive Panel

Our broadest blood work package yet, the Comprehensive Panel includes everything in the Advanced Panel, plus more detailed testing of liver and kidney function and nutritional balance. It offers a broader view of your body's key systems and how they are functioning.

Step 2Get Insights

Review your Results

Bring your results into focus. Explore the areas of your health covered by your panel, understand your starting point, and use your findings to guide a conversation with your provider.

  • Review results across key areas of your health
  • Prepare and review questions with your provider
  • Discuss medication options based on your results.
  • Review current medications and whether dosage adjustments may be appropriate.

Step 3Continuing Action

Track your Progress

You're making progress! Repeat your bloodwork every six months to follow your body’s changes, see trends over time, and track your achievements.

  • Compare results with your baseline over time
  • Reassess which medications remain appropriate as your health and goals evolve.
  • Continue to take steps to feel your absolute best
