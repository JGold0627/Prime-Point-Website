Questions we hear every day

What is Epitalon?

Epitalon is a synthetic peptide modeled after a substance associated with the pineal gland, which helps regulate the body’s sleep-wake cycle. Researchers are studying its potential connection to cellular aging and the body’s internal clock.

Who can benefit from Epitalon?

Researchers have studied middle-aged women who worked night shifts and had low melatonin production. Epitalon taken under the tongue changed the activity of a body-clock gene in their blood cells. A separate lab study found that Epitalon lengthened telomeres, the protective ends of chromosomes, in cultured human cells. Whether Epitalon injections improve sleep or help people live longer remains unknown.
