FAQ

Questions worth asking before you start.

A quick guide to eligibility, telehealth review, protocols, and what Prime Point means by clinician-guided peptide care.

Do I need a consult before accessing peptides?

Yes. Prime Point begins with a provider review to determine whether peptide care is appropriate for your goals and medical profile.

Are BPC-157 and TP-500 right for everyone?

No. A licensed provider must review health history, medications, and other factors before discussing any protocol.

Is the consult fully online?

Prime Point is designed as a telehealth experience, with intake, provider review, education, and follow-up handled remotely where legally available.

Will I get lifestyle guidance too?

Yes. Sleep, training, nutrition, stress, and routine all matter, so lifestyle support is part of the conversation.

Does this site provide medical advice?

No. Site content is educational. Medical decisions are made only through direct review with a licensed provider.

Peptide therapies require medical review and may not be appropriate for everyone. Information on this site is educational and is not a diagnosis, treatment claim, or guarantee of results.
