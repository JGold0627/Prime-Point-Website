About this peptide
GHK-Cu is a naturally occurring peptide that binds to copper and plays a role in the body's tissue maintenance and repair processes. It has attracted attention for its potential effects on skin health, collagen production, and hair growth, making it an area of interest in skin aging and regenerative research.
Potential function

GHK-Cu is being studied for its ability to influence collagen production, support skin structure, and promote the body's natural tissue repair processes. Early research also suggests it may affect hair follicle activity and help regulate inflammation, although these potential benefits have not been established for injectable use in humans.

Tissue remodeling
Skin tissue is continually broken down and rebuilt. Laboratory studies examine how GHK-Cu influences this process.

Questions we hear every day

What is GHK-Cu?

GHK-Cu is a naturally occurring copper-binding peptide found in the body. Researchers study its role in maintaining and repairing the tissue that gives skin its strength and structure.

Who can benefit from GHK-Cu?

People with diabetes who had ulcers on the soles of their feet saw a benefit in a trial of GHK-Cu gel. With the same wound care in both groups, ulcers treated with the gel shrank more than those treated with a placebo gel. Lab research also found that GHK-Cu stimulated collagen production. Whether injections offer a similar benefit for wound healing or skin health remains unclear.
