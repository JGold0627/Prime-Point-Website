Before you book

Good GLP-1 care should answer the important questions early.

Are GLP-1 medications automatic?

No. A provider reviews your health history, medications, goals, risks, and eligibility before any prescription is considered.

Will labs be required?

It depends. Existing labs may be reviewed, and additional labs may be recommended when they would support a safer decision.

What risks should be discussed?

Your provider can discuss side effects, medication conflicts, contraindications, follow-up needs, and realistic expectations.

What if GLP-1 care is not a fit?

Your next steps may include education, lifestyle guidance, monitoring priorities, labs, follow-up, or another care path.
