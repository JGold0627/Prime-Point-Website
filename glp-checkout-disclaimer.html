Before checkout

What happens next.

Your health questionnaire and identity information will be reviewed by a licensed Prime Point provider.

Your provider may contact you with questions before determining whether treatment is safe and appropriate for you. Prescriptions are never guaranteed and are issued only when clinically appropriate.

If you have not chosen a treatment option, your provider can discuss available options based on your health information and individual needs. After onboarding, you will be able to communicate with your care team through the patient portal.

Your card will not be charged at this time.

Adding a payment method authorizes Prime Point to process the applicable treatment charge only if a licensed provider prescribes treatment. Pricing and any recurring payment terms will be shown for your review before you submit payment information.
