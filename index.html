Who is Prime Point Health?

Prime Point Health brings personalized healthcare into the comfort of your home. Through our fully online process, we connect patients in all 50 states with licensed healthcare professionals and pharmacies. Whether you’re focused on daily wellness, weight management, or longevity, you can consult with a provider licensed in your state to explore care that fits your needs. When clinically appropriate, your provider may recommend prescription options, with medications conveniently delivered to your door.

What happens if I am not approved for medication?

If our medical providers do not prescribe your requested medication, we will notify you and issue a full refund for all costs incurred.

How long does it take to receive medication?

Once your medical intake and health history form have been reviewed and approved, you can expect your medication to arrive within 1–2 business days.

Does Prime Point Health require insurance?

No. Prime Point Health does not require insurance. Your medication, medical visit, and shipping are included in one clear, affordable price.

How do payments work?

Prime Point Health offers flexible payment options without a membership. If approved for medication, you can pay monthly or purchase a three-month supply delivered in a single shipment, depending on the medication. We also offer buy now, pay later options through our payment partners, giving you more flexibility in how you pay.
