FAQ

Common questions

What are peptides and how do they work?

Peptides are short chains of amino acids. In a consult, your provider can explain how different peptide topics may relate to your goals, health history, and care preferences.

What peptide topics can I discuss?

You can ask about goals, routines, medications, available labs, metabolic priorities, recovery needs, and whether any options are worth reviewing further.

Are peptides right for everyone?

No. Peptide-related care requires clinical review and may not be appropriate for every person, history, medication list, or set of goals.

How long does the review take?

Timing can vary. After you share your intake, the next step is a provider-led review of your goals, background, questions, and available health information.

Are prescriptions guaranteed?

No. Prescriptions are considered only when clinically appropriate after review, and eligibility, options, and next steps can vary.

Information on this site is educational and is not a diagnosis, treatment claim, or guarantee of results. Peptide therapies require clinical review, may not be appropriate for everyone, and are not guaranteed after a consult. Prescriptions are considered only when clinically appropriate.
