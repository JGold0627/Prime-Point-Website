What is MIC + B12?

MIC refers to methionine, inositol, and choline. MIC + B12 combines these compounds with vitamin B12. Formulations can vary, so your clinician and pharmacy should confirm the ingredients in any prescribed preparation.

How does MIC + B12 work?

Vitamin B12 is needed for healthy red blood cell formation, DNA synthesis, and nervous system function. Treating a B12 deficiency can address symptoms related to that deficiency, but B12 is not an established energy booster for people who already have adequate levels.
