Questions we hear every day

What is MOTS-c?

Encoded by mitochondrial DNA, MOTS-c is a 16-amino-acid peptide that acts as a messenger, helping cells switch on protective genes when glucose is scarce.

Who can benefit from MOTS-C?

Researchers have yet to establish who benefits from MOTS-C injections for blood sugar control or endurance. In mice with insulin resistance, MOTS-C helped muscles take up glucose in response to insulin. A cycling study in 10 young men found that exercise raised levels of their own naturally occurring MOTS-C. It did not test injections.
