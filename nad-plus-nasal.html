What is NAD+ Nasal?

NAD+ stands for nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide, a coenzyme involved in cellular energy metabolism. A nasal preparation delivers the formulation as a spray rather than an injection.

How does NAD+ Nasal work?

Within cells, NAD+ participates in reactions involved in energy metabolism and cellular maintenance. Its biological role does not establish that a nasal spray will improve energy, focus, or longevity. Findings from other formulations should not be assumed to apply to a nasal product.
