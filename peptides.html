WHAT IT IS

What is Peptide Therapy?

Peptide therapy uses targeted amino acid sequences to influence specific biological processes related to metabolism, recovery, and hormone signaling. By working at the cellular level, peptides can help support how the body functions over time, not just how it feels in the moment. At Prime Point Health, peptide therapy is used as part of a physician-guided plan designed to support recovery, performance, and long-term health.
