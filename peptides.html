Peptide Therapy FAQ

Explore clear answers about peptide-focused care, provider review, eligibility, and what to expect throughout the process.

Peptides are short chains of amino acids that act as signaling molecules in the body. Depending on the treatment plan, they may help support areas such as recovery, metabolism, energy, sleep, performance, and healthy aging.

Peptide therapy is designed to support specific biological pathways in the body. At Prime Point Health, every program begins with a physician-guided review to determine whether treatment is appropriate for your goals, health history, and overall wellness plan.

Peptide therapy may be appropriate for individuals looking to support recovery, energy, performance, body composition, metabolic health, or long-term wellness. Eligibility depends on your health history, goals, and provider review.

Lab work may be recommended depending on your goals, medical history, and the peptide therapy being considered. Prime Point Health uses provider review to help determine what is appropriate before treatment begins.

Many peptide therapies are administered through subcutaneous injection, while some may be available in other forms depending on the treatment. Your provider will review the appropriate method, instructions, and expectations if you are approved.

Timelines vary based on the individual, the peptide used, consistency, lifestyle, and the treatment goal. Some people may notice changes within weeks, while others may require more time and ongoing provider guidance.

Peptide therapy is not appropriate for everyone. Treatment should only be used under medical supervision. Prime Point Health uses physician-guided review to help assess eligibility, safety considerations, and ongoing support.
