Common questions

Practical answers before you request a consult.

Are peptides prescribed automatically after a consult?

No. A provider must review your health history, medications, goals, risks, and eligibility before any prescription is considered.

What information should I be ready to share?

Be ready to discuss your goals, medical history, current medications, allergies, routines, prior experiences, questions, and any recent labs or relevant health data.

Will I need lab work?

It depends. Existing labs may be reviewed if available, and your provider may recommend additional labs when they would help guide a safer care decision.

What risks or limitations should be discussed?

Your provider should discuss why a peptide topic may or may not fit your situation, possible side effects, medication conflicts, follow-up needs, and realistic expectations.

What happens if peptide therapy is not a fit?

Your provider can still help clarify other next steps, such as lifestyle priorities, labs, follow-up, or a different care path that better matches your health profile.

Information on this site is educational and is not a diagnosis, treatment claim, or guarantee of results. Peptide therapies require clinical review, may not be appropriate for everyone, and are not guaranteed after a consult. Prescriptions are considered only when clinically appropriate.
