How is this different from buying peptides online?

Many peptides sold online are labelled "research use only". Semax is only prescribed after an assessment with a Prime Point Health clinician, before it's prepared by a US-based licensed 503A pharmacy.

How does Semax work?

Semax functions by upregulating brain-derived neurotrophic factor (BDNF), which stimulates specific receptors to enhance synaptic plasticity and neural growth. It simultaneously accelerates the turnover and metabolism of dopamine and serotonin within the prefrontal cortex to improve focus and cognitive processing.

Questions we hear every day

What is Semax?

Semax is a synthetic peptide studied for its effects on the brain and nervous system. Research examines how it influences proteins that help nerve cells survive, adapt, and communicate.

Who can benefit from Semax?

For adults looking to improve focus, two human studies offer early evidence about Semax taken through the nose. One reported better performance on a work task. In the other, brain scans of 24 healthy volunteers showed short-term differences between Semax and placebo in a network active at rest. Whether these findings translate to better attention or memory in daily life remains unclear.
