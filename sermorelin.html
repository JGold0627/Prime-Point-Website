What is Sermorelin?

Sermorelin is a synthetic peptide that mimics growth hormone-releasing hormone (GHRH), a naturally occurring hormone involved in regulating the body's growth hormone production. Its use requires individualized medical evaluation to determine whether treatment is appropriate.

How does Sermorelin work?

Sermorelin acts on receptors in the pituitary gland, stimulating the release of the body's own growth hormone. Unlike administering growth hormone directly, sermorelin works through the body's existing hormonal signaling pathway. Treatment decisions should be based on a qualified clinician's assessment of the patient's medical history and individual needs.

Questions we hear every day

Is Sermorelin right for me?

Research suggests that Sermorelin can stimulate the body's own growth hormone release in some adults. Whether this is relevant to concerns such as energy, recovery, or body composition requires an individualized medical evaluation. It’s not recommended for those with active cancer, uncontrolled diabetes, severe sleep apnea, or complete GH deficiency requiring recombinant HGH. Your doctor will determine whether Sermorelin and its intended use are appropriate for you, based on your medical history, any necessary testing, and a review of potential benefits and risks.

How can Sermorelin therapy benefit me?

Feel more energized, sleep better, build lean muscle, burn fat more efficiently, and recover faster. Sermorelin may help adults with low GH levels achieve these benefits, with results often building over 2–3 months of consistent use.
